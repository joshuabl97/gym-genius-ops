### Two Deployment Models (Dec 2024)
**Model A: Hybrid (leverage existing cameras)**
- Y1 Margins: 49-60% depending on segment (with Ubiquiti AI 360 cameras)
- Hardware Cost: $2,365-$5,488 per tier
- Best for: Gyms with existing camera infrastructure

**Model B: Full Install (complete system)**
- Y1 Margins: 29-38% (lower due to hardware)
- Hardware Cost: $4,917-$38,195 per tier
- Best for: New gyms, major renovations

**Key Metrics:**
- Monthly COGS: $86/gym (cloud $26, support $40, maintenance $20)
- LTV: $39,473 at 12% annual churn (100-month lifetime)
- Pricing Formula: Base Fee ($246) + (Sqft × $0.0123) at 65% margin
- Default camera: Ubiquiti AI 360 @ $419 (sleek 360° fisheye)
