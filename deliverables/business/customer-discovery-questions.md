# Customer Discovery Questions

> **Goal:** Validate problem, solution, and pricing with 5-10 gym owners before building.
> **Format:** 20-30 minute calls. Listen more than pitch.

---

## Opening (2 min)

"Thanks for taking the time. I'm exploring a product idea for gym operators and want to learn about how you make equipment decisions. I'm not selling anything today—just learning."

---

## 1. Background (3 min)

| Question | Why We Ask |
|----------|------------|
| How many locations do you operate? | Segment size |
| What's your role? | Decision-maker validation |
| How long have you been in this role? | Experience level |

---

## 2. Equipment Decisions (8 min)

### Current Process

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Walk me through how you decided on your last major equipment purchase. | Process, data sources, gut vs. data |
| What information did you have when making that decision? | Current data gaps |
| What information do you wish you had? | **Pain validation** |

### Pain Points

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Have you ever bought equipment that turned out to be underused? | Direct pain confirmation |
| How did you find out it was underused? | Discovery lag time |
| What did that cost you? (money, member complaints, space) | **Quantified pain** |

### Current Solutions

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| How do you currently track which equipment gets used? | Existing solutions |
| What do you like about that approach? | Job-to-be-done |
| What's frustrating about it? | Gaps we can fill |

---

## 3. Decision Making (5 min)

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Who else is involved in equipment purchase decisions? | Buying committee |
| Do you need to justify equipment purchases to anyone? (investors, landlords, partners) | Reporting use case |
| What would make that justification easier? | Feature validation |

---

## 4. Cameras & Tech (4 min)

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Do you have security cameras in your facility? | BYOC eligibility |
| What brand/system? | Compatibility check |
| Do your cameras cover the equipment floor? | Coverage validation |
| Who manages your camera system? | IT decision-maker |

---

## 5. Solution Reaction (5 min)

**Brief pitch (30 seconds):**
"We're building a system that connects to your existing security cameras and shows you exactly which equipment gets used, when, and for how long. All processing happens locally—no video leaves your gym, no member tracking."

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| What's your initial reaction? | Interest level |
| What questions come to mind? | Objections, concerns |
| What would this need to do to be useful for you? | Must-have features |
| What would make you NOT want this? | Deal-breakers |

---

## 6. Pricing Reaction (3 min)

"We're thinking about pricing around $300-500/month depending on facility size, plus a one-time hardware cost."

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| How does that compare to what you'd expect? | Price anchoring |
| What would make that worth it? | Value drivers |
| What would make it not worth it? | Price objections |
| How do you typically budget for software/tools? | Budget process |

---

## 7. Closing (2 min)

| Question | What We're Listening For |
|----------|-------------------------|
| If this existed today, would you want to try it? | Intent signal |
| What would you need to see before signing up? | Proof points needed |
| Is there anyone else you think I should talk to? | Referrals |

**Ask permission:**
"Would it be okay if I followed up when we have something to show?"

## Red Flags to Listen For

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| "We just count heads" | May not care about equipment-level data |
| "My staff walks the floor" | Current solution "good enough" |
| "We don't really buy equipment" | Not our customer |
| "Privacy concerns" | Need strong privacy story |
| "That's too expensive" | Price sensitivity, may not value |

---

## Green Lights to Listen For

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| "I wish I knew that before buying X" | Direct pain |
| "We spent $X on equipment that nobody uses" | Quantified pain |
| "My investors ask about utilization" | Reporting use case |
| "We have Meraki/Ubiquiti cameras" | BYOC ready |
| "When can I try it?" | Strong intent |
